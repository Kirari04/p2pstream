The current route-locality preference preserves concurrency spreading by claiming only exact full-key idle entries with inFlight==0 under tunnelMu then pool.mu. A successful claim increments inFlight before either lock is released; response-body completion owns its eventual decrement. Other concurrent requests cannot claim that entry and fall through to ordinary round-robin lane selection without waiting. Cold targets have no eligible entries, so they use the same round robin. Factory construction itself does not advance/claim/allocate. Aggregate mode starts at bulk lane1, excluding reserved control; environments and trusted one-shot/control selection are unchanged. Successful idle preference advances the shared RR cursor. Four full-key lookups bound preference; additional lanes retain ordinary RR outside the current preference window. No lock spans dialing or HTTP I/O.

Same-key sequential reuse intentionally favors the most recently used idle entry; this is not a promise of uniform lane utilization for sequential traffic. Full key contains agent identity, route target, origin, TLS policy and response timeout; eligibility also compares the live agent pointer and retired/session state. Different targets cannot claim each other's entries. Agent generation replacement, route target eviction and closeAll retirement remain separate exclusion paths.
