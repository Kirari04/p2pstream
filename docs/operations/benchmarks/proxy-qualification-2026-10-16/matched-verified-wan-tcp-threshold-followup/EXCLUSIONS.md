# Selection and omissions

Only exact frozen source, invocation, bounded evidence records, native envelopes, handoffs, model policy/escalation and immutable final review are selected. All mapped evidence is loose; no archive or compression is used. The original history package remains a separately published immutable cutoff.

Fresh normal/race Go caches, module workspace and temporary directories are outside the evidence records boundary. OMITTED-ROOTS.tsv identifies those roots and the authoritative records ledger; it does not claim to hash or cover cache payload contents. Copied audited source and binary payloads are excluded and remain bound by the referenced immutable source/build chain. No workspace/cache traversal or payload hash scan is performed for omission metadata.

TINY1-OMISSIONS.plan.tsv and TINY2-OMISSIONS.plan.tsv are exact original private-artifact reference tables for the nonprinting scan. Their omitted key, readiness/control and raw-log bytes are never copied into this supplement. The mutable main review log is excluded; only an explicit immutable final cutoff snapshot may be bound.
