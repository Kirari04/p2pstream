# Fixed 256 MiB controller workload: causal-design/source review

Reviewed HEAD: `0ed06e74129db4bf9e8b3d3394d3d986fe3cf22e`.
Scope: finite source/evidence review only. No implementation, builds, tests, validator execution, traffic, or production changes. Input hashes are in `INPUTS.json`.

## Decision

**GO for one separately preregistered source derivative that retains fully evidenced post-warmup origin-reuse outcomes in every arm. NO-GO for executing the existing vector runner with its conditioning check merely removed, or for calling a successful C/R/C screen proof of a production fix.** Root owns subsequent implementation/runtime decisions.

The change in question is scientifically meaningful. The previous experiment asked about a comparison conditional on retained origins. The new question asks what changing the data-lane controller does to the fixed, sequential, full-size workload, including its downstream origin reconnections. Both questions can be legitimate, but they have different estimands. The historical rejected cohorts remain rejected under their own plans. They are discovery evidence, never fresh controls for the new plan.

In the fresh rejected CUBIC observation, sequence 14 took 16.93725726 seconds; later visits 15/16 occurred after 34.042501953/34.508404577 seconds and explicitly reported new origins with `reused=false`. A plausible causal path is controller -> transfer duration -> time since prior use -> idle expiration -> later transfer behavior. Conditioning on retained reuse blocks that path and can preferentially exclude slow arms. Excluding Reno arms for failing throughput floors creates a second, direct outcome-dependent selection. The new design must remove both exclusions from arm continuation/comparability, symmetrically. Performance and fully evidenced origin reuse become outcomes; they do not disappear from the mature attestation.

This is an intervention on entire fresh workloads, with intention-to-treat-style accounting. Fixed C/R/C is not randomized ITT: controller and arm order are confounded, and there is one Reno arm. Bracketing controls offer a useful finite screen against simple drift, not identification of a population causal effect. A positive result supports a controller-responsive workload under this setup and warrants follow-up; it does not establish HyStart, the threshold creator, H2 correctness, production Reno suitability, or the original 96-transfer WAN requirement.

## Precisely define the new population and outcomes

The intervention begins before TLS on data lanes 1–4, **before the eight growth warmups**. Consequently, the pool-growth barrier before row 9 is an initial measurement condition, not a pretreatment covariate. Failure to reach it may itself be controller-dependent. Retain such a failure in the attempted-arm ledger, stop under the unchanged validity/resource rules, and report an incomplete diagnostic without replacing the arm. Do not describe the resulting completed-arm contrast as unconditional ITT over all possible startup/growth failures.

The supported connection outcome is upstream origin HTTP connection replacement/new Yamux stream on an unchanged, independently owned tunnel TCP lane. `validate_bindings` currently requires five exact lane/generation/tuple/session identities throughout. An actual tunnel TCP owner/session replacement remains outside the supported measurement contract: retain it as a failed attempted-arm outcome, do not silently omit it, and do not claim a throughput effect for an unobserved replacement owner. The proposed experiment needs no broader TCP capture.

The finite screen therefore estimates complete-workload controller effects on measured post-warmup transfers and origin churn **when all required acquisition, payload, ownership and resource contracts remain valid**. Failure outcomes remain in the report, but the existing instrumentation cannot turn missing/invalid measurements into numerical performance estimates. Retention is not the same as declaring a failed acquisition comparable.

## A concrete, nonselective decision rule

Use one complete comparison after all three measurement-valid arms. Evaluate every criterion, even when earlier criteria fail; retain all values.

* **Workload signal:** Reno's four-download pooled rate is at least 108% of the faster whole CUBIC control. Compare against both controls individually in the report.
* **No concealed post-warmup regression:** every row 9–16 in Reno is at least 95% of the faster corresponding CUBIC row. This explicitly includes uploads; do not insert an absolute upload floor that the previous plan never had.
* **Read concordance:** report whether the sum of four Reno upstream-Read durations is strictly below both CUBIC sums. Treat this as mechanism-concordance evidence, not arm eligibility. A rate signal without Read concordance remains a measured workload signal with unexplained attribution, not permission to claim the previously hypothesized Read mechanism.
* **Floor restoration:** independently report whether Reno satisfies every original download floor (1–4, 9, 11, 14, 15) and all mature performance predicates. This is a success outcome, never an inclusion or continuation rule. A positive rate signal that still misses floors does not fix the regression. Origin-reuse conditioning can keep mature acceptance false even when performance floors are restored.
* **Reproduction and stability:** report which original floors each CUBIC control misses, both control vectors and pooled control/control ratio. Do not require a particular 11/14 failure pattern, or discard a healthy/brisk control to manufacture reproduction. If both controls are healthy, an observed speedup does not demonstrate repair of a reproduced failure. Strong disagreement between controls weakens attribution; it does not erase the data or authorize another attempt.

Call the result a **promising diagnostic lead** only when the workload-signal and no-regression rules pass; label Read concordance, floor restoration and control reproduction independently. Otherwise report the failed criteria and direction/magnitude of the observed contrast. Never equate failure of a conservative 108%/95% decision rule with proof of no controller effect. These numerical thresholds are screening conventions preserved/extended from the prior proposal, not statistical guarantees or WAN qualification thresholds.

Keeping original Read concordance and all-download-floor requirements as additional final success criteria is also valid if root prefers continuity; they must not suppress the effect report or stop the last arm. Freeze that choice before runtime. The concrete plan recommended here separates measured effect, explanatory concordance and operational usefulness.

## Existing source supports a narrow derivative

The mature `validate-wan-below-tls-arm.py` already separates malformed/missing revisit evidence (integrity) from fully evidenced reuse loss (conditioning), lines 287–349; performance/conditioning partitions and runner status are retained by `validate-arm-outcome.py`. `validate-controller-arm-integrity.py:evaluate` validates that partition, natural ownership, namespace, cgroup, terminal attribution and selected sender joins while returning `conditioning_passed` separately. Its `accepted` can be true with conditioning-only failures. **Reuse that helper unchanged, requiring its `accepted`, not its separate `conditioning_passed`.** This preserves mature failures and existing terminal/legacy-106 behavior.

The current vector code adds four incompatible restrictions: `validate_arm` requires `closure['conditioning_passed']`; `decide_arm` requires an empty conditioning list and equates mature acceptance with absence of performance issues; its Reno branch rejects on mature acceptance/floors; `decide_screen` requires Reno mature acceptance and assumes all eligible attestations have no conditioning failure. The current launcher aborts whenever arm decision validation exits nonzero. Replacing only the first condition is insufficient.

**One next source plan:** add new `validate-controller-workload.py`, new focused offline contracts, and a fresh `run-controller-workload.py` plus separately frozen plan/commands/input manifest. Leave every existing mature/vector validator, old plan, proof and output unchanged. Copy or compose the reviewed vector code only in the new derivative:

Keep `validate_bindings` and the existing integrity helper unmodified. Preserve source/binary hashes, selected copy/TCP/terminal joins and all resource/natural-ownership checks.
2. Add a pure raw-evidence classifier for the new reuse outcomes. ## Focused offline negative controls before considering runtime

* Replay immutable fixtures for the two historical cohorts into the new **classifier only**: demonstrate that their old attestation remains byte/semantic-identical and false; the later cohort's fully evidenced 15/16 replacements become reported outcomes. This is a contract fixture, not historical control reuse or revised acceptance under its old plan.
* The same valid replacement at each sequence 13–16, under each controller, is classified consistently. Slow or below-floor Reno remains measurement-valid and cannot suppress final-control scheduling. Equal throughput with different valid churn produces no throughput-effect claim. A slow CUBIC with valid churn remains in pooled denominators.
* Missing/wrong origin, missing/duplicate trace, contradictory reuse/origin flags, invalid timestamps, negative elapsed time, malformed bool/int, missing PutIdleConn, wrong lane/key, controller readback mismatch, owner generation/session replacement, failed initial freshness and extra pool-conditioning issue all reject. Do not reject valid connection behavior merely because its timing crosses a guessed deterministic timer boundary.
* Existing integrity, payload/event, TLS/protocol, capture-bound, resource, namespace, adopted-descendant, terminal and forced-cleanup failures continue to reject; outcome labels cannot override them. An injected message matching an allowed conditioning string without its raw evidence must fail.
* Exact 108% and 95% boundaries and adjacent representable failures; wrong arm order, absent row, duplicate row, omitted slow row, NaN/infinity/zero/bool duration or rate, altered original attestation, forged saved decision and read-total mismatch fail as appropriate. Verify all-three evaluation with a failing Reno floor, and that incomplete arms yield no effect estimate.

## Alternative and stopping boundary

A prospectively randomized, replicated series of whole fresh workloads would improve causal identification and precision because arm order is then not deterministically tied to controller. It needs a separate fixed sample/budget and decision plan; it is not justification to append favorable repetitions to this C/R/C run. Smaller bodies, hard under-idle deadlines or resetting connections would alter the very history and mediator now being studied, so they are less informative for this 256 MiB question.

The immediate next step is the narrow source derivative and offline proof above. The evidence supports no production congestion-control, timeout, HTTP window, buffer, scheduler or transport policy change. Original WAN qualification remains failed/unresolved, and this review grants no HAProxy, parity, merge or release clearance.
